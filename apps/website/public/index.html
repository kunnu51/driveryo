Our Vision & Mission

About DRIVERYO

Transforming urban car ownership into an effortless chauffeur-driven experience.

Founded in Hyderabad, DRIVERYO was established with a singular vision: to empower private car owners with effortless chauffeur services tailored strictly to their own vehicles.

Millions of Indian households invest significantly in comfortable personal cars. Yet, navigating relentless Hyderabad traffic, managing parking headaches at crowded venues, or enduring exhaustion on highway journeys frequently diminishes that joy.

Driveryo connects car owners directly with vetted, polite, background-checked professional drivers who treat your vehicle with meticulous care.
